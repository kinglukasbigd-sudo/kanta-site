How it works

Spot it. Report it. Watch it get fixed.

1

Spot a full or damaged container

Open the map. Every container in the city is there, in the colour of its state.

2

Report it in 20 seconds

Camera first, three taps. When a neighbour confirms it, the marker changes for everyone, and for the city.

3

Watch the city fix it

You get a quiet "Fixed!" notice with before and after photos when it is emptied or repaired.

Read the map

One badge, one state.

What you can do

  • Find the nearest container with space

    Within 600 m, with walking time and directions, the moment yours is full.

  • Confirm what neighbours reported

    "Me too" or "It's been emptied". The map stays honest because people check it.

  • Suggest a new container

    Mark a spot Skopje needs one, and vote for other people's suggestions.

  • Map your street

    Add a container that is missing from the map, or mark one that is gone.

  • City stats

    How quickly each municipality fixes problems. Data, not blame.

  • Works offline

    No signal at the container? The report is kept and sent when you are back online.

Private by design

Your photo helps. Your name stays yours.

Faces blurred

On your phone, before the photo is sent. Hidden photo data, GPS included, is removed.

"A neighbour"

That is all other people ever see. Your name and email are never shown.

No password

Browse without an account. To report, sign in with your email and a 6-digit code.

Delete anytime

In Settings. Your reports stay on the map, anonymously. No ads.
